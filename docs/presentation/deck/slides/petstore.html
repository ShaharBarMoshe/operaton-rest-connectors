Unmodified petstore3.swagger.io (OpenAPI 3.0.4) and petstore.swagger.io/v2 (Swagger 2.0), referenced by component and by POST /pet: 12 cases, plus a BPMN task with the whole 17 KB document. Broken values are reported by path, e.g. /tags/0/id.

Pet has a nested object, category, and an array of nested objects, tags, both by $ref. The two inputs merge at every level: category gets its id from the first input and its name from the second. Grey fields are not in the schema and are not sent; "10" is coerced to the declared integer. The documents are in src/test/resources/openapi under their Apache 2.0 licence.
